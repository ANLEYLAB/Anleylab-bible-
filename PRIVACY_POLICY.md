## Overview

ANLEYLAB ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. The **ANLEYLAB Bible** app is designed to provide access to the 73-book Amharic Catholic Holy Bible completely **offline**, with zero tracking, data harvesting, or user monitoring.

This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles your data.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use

**ANLEYLAB Bible does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.**

- **No Personal Information:** We do not require or collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or contact list.
- **No Device Identifiers or Location Data:** We do not collect device identifiers (IMEI, Android ID, Advertising ID) or location data.
- **No Account Required:** You do not need to register, log in, or create an account to use any feature of the application.
- **No Analytics or Tracking:** We do not use third-party analytics services (such as Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, etc.) or tracking tools.
- **No Advertisements:** ANLEYLAB Bible is free of commercial advertisements and ad networks.

---

## 2. Local Data Storage

All application features—including your personal highlights, bookmarks, notes, font size preferences, theme choices, and reading history—are stored **strictly locally on your device**.

- Your notes, bookmarks, and preferences never leave your device.
- We do not have access to your saved notes or reading history.
- Uninstalling the application or clearing local app data will permanently remove your stored notes and bookmarks from your device.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services & External Links

- The application contains an optional external website link (`www.anleylab.et`). Tapping this link opens your device's web browser to visit our official website.
- When visiting external websites, those sites operate under their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any external websites you visit.

---

## 4. Children's Privacy

ANLEYLAB Bible does not collect any personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The app is suitable for users of all ages (Content Rating: Everyone / 3+).

---

## 5. Security

Because all app data remains stored locally on your device and no data is transmitted over the internet, your information is protected by your own device's built-in security mechanisms.
